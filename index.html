About Field Viewer

Field Viewer is a free browser sketch of electron and photon fields. Electrons, positrons, and photons are wave packets. When an electron and a positron meet, the app replaces them with two photons and shows the energy and momentum before and after. It is a teaching sketch with stated simplifications, not a research simulator. The About tab covers the controls, the model, and what it leaves out.
